# OpenMarket AI Agent — Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment

OpenMarket AI Agent is a free, open-source, community-driven initiative dedicated to making financial market intelligence accessible to everyone.

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for contributors from around the world.

Our community welcomes participation regardless of nationality, geographic location, age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, personal appearance, religion, or other personal characteristics.

All participants are expected to follow this Code of Conduct.

## 2. Our Core Values

### Respect

Treat every contributor with dignity and professionalism.

### Collaboration

Encourage constructive teamwork, knowledge sharing, and mutual learning.

### Transparency

Communicate honestly about project decisions, technical limitations, and research findings.

### Scientific Integrity

Prioritize reproducible research, evidence-based analysis, and responsible financial communication.

### Accessibility

Help make technical knowledge understandable and accessible to people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

### Accountability

Take responsibility for your contributions, decisions, and interactions with others.

## 3. Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.
- Welcome newcomers and less experienced contributors.
- Provide useful technical feedback.
- Respect different perspectives and cultural backgrounds.
- Acknowledge mistakes and correct them responsibly.
- Credit the work of other contributors.
- Respect privacy, security, and intellectual property.
- Follow applicable laws and platform policies.
- Keep discussions focused on the project's mission.
- Resolve disagreements through evidence and professional dialogue.

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following conduct is not permitted:

- Harassment, intimidation, or bullying.
- Discrimination or hateful language.
- Personal attacks, threats, or abusive comments.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate content.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading financial performance claims.
- Fabricating research results or market data.
- Promoting financial scams or fraudulent schemes.
- Spam, malicious links, or unauthorized advertising.
- Deliberate disruption of community discussions.
- Retaliation against people who report misconduct.

## 5. Responsible Financial Communication

Because OpenMarket AI Agent focuses on financial markets, contributors must maintain high standards of honesty and transparency.

Contributors must not:

- Promise guaranteed investment returns.
- Present unverified strategies as proven profitable systems.
- Manipulate research results to attract users.
- Conceal known risks or significant limitations.
- Misrepresent simulated performance as live trading results.
- Encourage market manipulation or fraudulent activity.

Research discussions should distinguish facts, hypotheses, predictions, and independently verified results.

## 6. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests
- Repository discussions and code reviews
- Official project communication channels
- Community meetings and events
- Public interactions when representing the project

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

Project leadership will establish a dedicated confidential reporting channel before expanding community operations.

Until that channel is available, avoid posting sensitive personal information in public GitHub Issues.

Reports should include relevant context and evidence when safely available.

## 8. Enforcement

Project maintainers may respond to violations through:

1. Private guidance or corrective feedback.
2. Formal warnings.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal of harmful content.
5. Permanent removal from project community spaces when necessary.

Actions will be based on the severity, context, and recurrence of the behavior.

Project maintainers will seek to handle reports fairly, consistently, and confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.
